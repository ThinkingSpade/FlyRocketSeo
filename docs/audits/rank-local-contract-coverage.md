Primary provider contracts checked for semantics:

- [Organic SERP fields](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/serp/google/organic/live/advanced/) distinguish absolute positions from result-type group positions; an organic absolute position is not forcibly clamped to the requested organic depth.
- [Local Finder observations](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/serp/google/local_finder/live/advanced/) provide result group and absolute ranks.
- [Google review results](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/business_data/google/reviews/task_get/) document five-star ratings and timestamped sampled reviews.
- [Bulk backlink ranks](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/backlinks/bulk_ranks/live/) support an explicitly requested hundred-point scale; [spam scores](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/backlinks/bulk_spam_score/live/) are provider review signals.
- [New/lost timeseries](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/backlinks/timeseries_new_lost_summary/live/) use full grouped periods and provider-returned observations. The application adds no replacement dates or fabricated counts.
